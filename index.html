Copy your signature, then paste it into your email settings

  1. Open Settings → See all settings → General.
  2. Under Signature, click Create new, then paste your copied signature into the editor.
  3. Choose your signature defaults, then click Save Changes.
Gmail’s setup guide
  1. On your Apple device, open Mail → Settings → Signatures. Select your account and click +.
  2. Turn off Always match my default message font, then replace the default text with your copied signature.
  3. Select your new signature from the Choose Signature menu.
Apple Mail’s setup guide
Outlook for Mac
  1. Open Outlook → Settings → Signatures.
  2. Click +, name your signature, and paste it into the signature editor.
  3. Under Set default signature, choose your account and signature for new messages, replies, and forwards.
  4. Close the Signatures window. Repeat the default selection for any other accounts that need the signature.
Microsoft’s Mac setup guide
New Outlook / web
  1. In new Outlook for Windows or Outlook on the web, open Settings → Accounts → Signatures.
  2. Select Add signature, name it, and paste your copied signature into the editor.
  3. Choose whether to include it in new messages, replies, and forwards.
  4. Click Save.
Microsoft’s setup guide
Classic Outlook for Windows
  1. Open File → Options → Mail → Signatures.
  2. Click New, enter a name, then paste your copied signature into Edit signature.
  3. Under Choose default signature, select your email account and choose the signature for New messages and Replies/forwards.
  4. Click Save, then OK. Open a new message to check the result.
Microsoft’s setup guide

For other email apps, paste your signature into the signature editor with rich-text formatting enabled.
