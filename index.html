Copy your signature, then paste it into your email settings

  1. Open Settings → See all settings → General.
  2. Under Signature, click Create new, then paste your copied signature into the editor.
  3. Choose your signature defaults, then click Save Changes.
Gmail’s setup guide
  1. On your Mac, open Mail → Settings → Signatures. Select your account and click +.
  2. Turn off Always match my default message font, then replace the default text with your copied signature.
  3. Select your new signature from the Choose Signature menu.
Apple Mail’s setup guide
  1. In the new Outlook app or Outlook on the web, open Settings → Accounts → Signatures.
  2. Create a new signature, then paste your copied signature into the editor.
  3. Select your defaults and click Save.

Classic Outlook: File → Options → Mail → Signatures.

Outlook’s setup guide

For other email apps, paste your signature into the signature editor with rich-text formatting enabled.
